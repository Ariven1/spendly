Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

01Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect bank account details or payment card numbers.

02How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly for you: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you see in the app. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

03Data Storage

Your data is stored in our application database and is accessible only to your account. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may ask us to delete your account, and your stored data will be removed along with it.

04Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your expense data with any third party, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

05Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to, or deletion of, your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
